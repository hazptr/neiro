Neiro

Mihon, with background music for sources that provide it. Everything else is Mihon, and your extensions keep working.

Download Extension store

Android 8.0 or newer. Installs next to Mihon. Pop some bubbles while youre here.

What it adds

Music that comes with the chapter

♪

Background music

Each track plays over the pages its cued to, with a crossfade between tracks.

◐

Plays nice with other audio

Whatever else is playing gets quieter instead of stopping.

⤓

Works offline

Tracks get saved with downloaded chapters, so they play without a connection.

⟲

Mute and repeat

Right in the reader menu, top right corner, whenever a chapter has music.

⧉

Your extensions

Regular extensions work exactly like they do in Mihon.

↻

Updates itself

New versions show up in the app with whats changed.

Setup

Three steps

Install Neiro

Grab the APK from the latest release and open it.

Add the extension store

Settings, Browse, Extension stores. Extensions that play music have a note on their icon.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hazptr/neiro-extensions/repo/index.min.json

Turn the music on

In the reader settings, General, Play background music. Its off by default.

Built on Mihon, Apache-2.0. Icon glyph from M PLUS Rounded 1c (OFL).
